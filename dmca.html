Copyright / DMCA

Amazing Video respects intellectual property rights. Copyright owners may submit notices concerning content they believe infringes their rights.

Copyright Complaints

Include sufficient information to identify the copyrighted work and the allegedly infringing material.

1. Copyright Policy

Content made available through Amazing Video should not violate the copyright or intellectual property rights of others.

When we receive a sufficiently complete and valid copyright complaint, the relevant content may be reviewed and access may be restricted where appropriate.

2. Information Required in a Copyright Notice

A copyright complaint should include:

  • Your full legal name and contact information.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  • The exact URL of the content being reported.
  • Enough information to locate the material.
  • A statement explaining your good-faith belief that the use is not authorized.
  • A statement that the information supplied is accurate and that you are authorized to act on behalf of the rights holder.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

3. Misrepresentation

Submitting knowingly false or misleading copyright claims may have legal consequences.

Only submit a copyright complaint when you genuinely believe you own, represent or are otherwise authorized to enforce the relevant rights.

4. Review Process

After a complaint is received, we may review the identified material and supporting information.

Where appropriate, access to the identified content may be disabled or further information may be requested.

5. Counter Notification

Where applicable, a person whose content has been disabled may be able to provide information explaining why they believe removal was made in error.

Any counter-notification should contain complete and accurate contact information and sufficient details concerning the disputed material.

6. Repeat Infringement

Repeated copyright violations may result in additional restrictions where appropriate.
