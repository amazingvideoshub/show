Content Standards

Content Policy

This policy explains what types of content are permitted and prohibited on Amazing Video.

18+

Adult-Only Platform

Age-restricted content must involve adults only and must comply with consent, safety, ownership and applicable legal requirements.

1. Adults Only

  • All individuals depicted in age-restricted content must be adults.
  • Content involving minors is prohibited.
  • Age-ambiguous sexualized content is prohibited.
  • Users must comply with local age-access laws.

2. Consent

Content involving people must be consensual.

  • Participants must have knowingly consented to the relevant activity.
  • Distribution of private intimate material without consent is prohibited.
  • Hidden-camera or secretly recorded intimate material is prohibited.

3. Strictly Prohibited Content

  • Child sexual exploitation or abuse material.
  • Sexual content involving minors.
  • Non-consensual intimate imagery.
  • Sexual violence or coercion.
  • Human trafficking or sexual exploitation.
  • Illegal recordings or unlawful distribution.
  • Content that violates applicable criminal law.

4. Violence and Harm

Content primarily intended to promote serious violence, criminal harm or abuse may be prohibited.

Material depicting illegal conduct may also be restricted where required for safety or legal compliance.

5. Copyrighted Content

Users and content providers must have appropriate rights or authorization for content made available through the platform.

Copyright owners may submit a complaint through our Copyright / DMCA page.

6. Privacy

  • Do not publish private personal information without authorization.
  • Do not expose private addresses, passwords or sensitive credentials.
  • Do not use the platform for blackmail, intimidation or doxxing.

7. Malware and Deceptive Content

  • Malware or malicious downloads.
  • Phishing links.
  • Fake login pages.
  • Fraudulent redirects.
  • Content intended to compromise users' devices or accounts.

8. Content Review and Removal

Content may be disabled or removed when a valid report, copyright complaint, safety concern or legal issue is identified.

Serious violations may also result in further restrictions or referral where required by applicable law.
