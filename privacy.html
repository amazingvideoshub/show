Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Amazing Video handles information when you access or interact with our website.

Privacy First

We aim to collect only the information reasonably required to operate, secure and improve the service.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use Amazing Video, information may include:

  • Basic technical information such as browser, device type and operating system.
  • IP address and approximate region where technically necessary for security or service operation.
  • Information voluntarily submitted through report or contact forms.
  • Website preferences saved locally in your browser.

2. Age Verification Preference

Amazing Video uses browser storage such as localStorage to remember your age-verification selection.

For example, the website may save whether you confirmed that you are 18 or older or whether access should remain restricted on that device.

This local preference is stored within your browser and is used to control access to age-restricted pages.

3. How Information May Be Used

  • Operate and maintain the website.
  • Provide requested content and functionality.
  • Protect the platform against abuse, malicious activity and security threats.
  • Process reports, copyright requests and support inquiries.
  • Improve website performance and reliability.

4. External Video and Content Providers

Some video files, thumbnails or other media displayed on Amazing Video may be hosted by third-party services.

When your browser requests content from those third-party services, those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser information and request details according to their own privacy policies.

5. Cookies and Browser Storage

Amazing Video may use cookies or browser storage for essential preferences, security, age verification and website functionality.

You can clear browser storage or cookies through your browser settings. Doing so may reset stored preferences.

6. Information Sharing

We do not intend to sell personal information. Information may be disclosed where reasonably necessary:

  • To operate services through authorized providers.
  • To investigate abuse or security incidents.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.
  • To protect users, rights, safety or property.

7. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures may be used to protect information.

However, no website, network or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Adults Only

Amazing Video is intended exclusively for adults aged 18 years or older.

The service is not intended for children or minors. If information relating to a minor is discovered, appropriate action may be taken.

9. Your Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning personal information you have submitted, including requesting correction or deletion.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when website functionality, practices or legal requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page.
