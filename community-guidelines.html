Safe Community

Community Guidelines

These guidelines explain the standards that apply to content, users and activity on Amazing Video. They are designed to help maintain a lawful, respectful and age-appropriate platform.

By accessing or using Amazing Video, you agree to follow these Community Guidelines, our Terms, Privacy Policy and applicable laws. Content or activity that violates these rules may be restricted or removed.

18+

Adults Only

Amazing Video is intended only for users who are at least 18 years old and legally permitted to access age-restricted content in their location.

01

Age and Eligibility

Users must meet the minimum legal age required to access this platform.

  • Users must be 18 years of age or older.
  • Users must comply with applicable local laws and age restrictions.
  • Never upload, request or distribute content involving minors.
  • Content must not present a minor or age-ambiguous person in a sexual context.
02

Consent and Personal Safety

Content involving people must respect consent, privacy and personal safety.

  • Only content involving consenting adults is permitted.
  • Do not upload private or intimate material without permission from the people shown.
  • Hidden-camera, secretly recorded or non-consensual intimate material is prohibited.
  • Do not threaten, blackmail or pressure another person to provide content.
03

Prohibited Content

Certain categories of content are not permitted on Amazing Video.

  • Any sexual content involving minors.
  • Non-consensual or exploitative intimate content.
  • Sexual violence, coercion or abuse.
  • Content promoting trafficking or exploitation.
  • Illegal content or activity.
  • Content encouraging serious violence, self-harm or dangerous criminal activity.
04

Respectful Behaviour

Treat other users and people represented in content with respect.

  • Do not harass, threaten or intimidate users.
  • Do not share another person's private information without permission.
  • Impersonation or deceptive identity use is not permitted.
  • Do not repeatedly target individuals with abusive or unwanted messages.
05

Copyright and Ownership

Only share material you own or are legally authorized to distribute.

  • Respect copyrights and other intellectual property rights.
  • Do not repost copyrighted material without appropriate permission.
  • Valid copyright complaints may result in content removal.
  • Repeated infringement may result in further access restrictions.
06

Spam, Malware and Abuse

Do not use Amazing Video to harm users, manipulate the service or distribute unsafe material.

  • Malware, viruses or malicious downloads.
  • Phishing or credential theft.
  • Automated spam or misleading links.
  • Attempts to interfere with website security.
  • Fraudulent or intentionally deceptive activity.
07

Reporting Content

If you believe content violates these guidelines, please report it so that it can be reviewed.

  • Provide the relevant video URL or content ID.
  • Clearly describe the reason for your report.
  • Provide accurate information when submitting a complaint.
  • Emergency or criminal matters should also be reported to the appropriate authorities.

Zero-Tolerance Safety Rules

Amazing Video does not permit content involving child sexual exploitation, non-consensual intimate imagery, trafficking, exploitation or other unlawful abuse.

  • Content involving minors in a sexual context is strictly prohibited.
  • Non-consensual intimate material is strictly prohibited.
  • Content involving exploitation or trafficking is strictly prohibited.
  • Serious violations may be preserved or reported where required by applicable law.
